## Designed but NOT applied to paper — threshold reform for synthetic archetypes

The current sec5 Table 2 uses hand-set thresholds (ε_dom = ε_signal = 0.15, ε_close = 0.5) that are not justified. We designed a principled replacement.

### Principle

ε = 1.645 · σ_max where σ_max is the largest bootstrap SE across all measured Δ scores at the production sample budget. This is the one-sided 95% threshold appropriate for the directional desiderata; the multiplier is the Z-quantile so that under a null Δ=0, fewer than 5% of measurements would exceed ε.

### Sample budget bumped to 20 000

5k samples ⇒ σ_max ≈ 0.18 (threshold would be at the noise level, defeating the point). At 20k, σ_max ≈ 0.092 ⇒ ε ≈ 0.15 (coincidentally the same as the old hand-set value, but now defended).

### Desiderata dropped

- **#11** (P quantitatively matches L₁ in C2): measured −0.27, CI [−0.44, −0.11] — gap clearly nonzero. The structural symmetry claim doesn't hold at production budget (likely sampler regime distribution; L₁ stays in even when L₂ intervention turns gate off, P doesn't).
- **#12** (cross-regime P flip): measured 0.14, CI [−0.08, 0.37] — straddles zero, not significant. Replaced by prose ("flip is the conjunction of #8 and #10").

### Desiderata kept (10/10 pass at ε ≈ 0.15, 20 k samples)

### Forest plot designed

`figures/archetypes_threshold_forest.png` — final design (v8):
- Each row: plain-language structural claim, e.g. "Heaviest linear contributor L₂ has the largest necessity (gate-off regime)"
- Dot = point estimate; horizontal bar = bootstrap 95% CI
- Per-row **green pass region** (differs by claim type: x > ε, x > 0, or |x| < ε)
- Right-column annotation shows formula + value, e.g. `ΔS(O₂) − ΔS(O₁) = +0.347`
- Despined; ordered #1..#10; legend below plot

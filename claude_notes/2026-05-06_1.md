## Open thread — abduction in the formal definition

Long discussion about whether abduction is part of the PCI definition.

**Key finding**: Definition 8 (`def:jointnecsuf`) integrates over $P_\mathbf{U}$
(prior), not $P(\mathbf{U} | \text{obs})$ (posterior). Strictly speaking, the
definition does NOT abduct — both worlds share the same noise realization $u$,
drawn from the prior, with $u$ marginalised over.

For OBCB this is invisible: restoring Alice's factual values gives $Y' (u) = $
loan denial under every $u$, so abduction-vs-prior is a label dispute.

For the continuous signal example the two diverge sharply. The paper's signal
computation in `sec4_examples.tex` claims "partial abduction: factual noise is
abducted in the sufficiency world," giving $|Y^s - y^\star| = 0$. Following the
strict definition (no abduction, integrate over $P_\mathbf{U}$):

| Instance | $ci(X \to Y)$ no-abd | $ci(M \to Y)$ no-abd |
|---|---|---|
| I1 ($x=m=y=1$) | $0.677 - 0.357 = +0.320$ | $0.677 - 0.252 = +0.425$ |
| I2 ($x=m=y=0.5$) | $0.534 - 0.357 = +0.177$ | $0.534 - 0.252 = +0.282$ |
| I3 ($x=-0.75, m=y=1$) | $0.677 - 1.750 = -1.073$ | $0.677 - 0.252 = +0.425$ |

Without abduction:
- Zero desiderata (DYX, DYM, DMX) all hold structurally
- DMXY satisfied **without witnesses** (sufficiency variance for $M$ is 0.1 vs
  0.2 for $X$; necessity is identical) — this is a striking simplification
- ci becomes signed; $X$ is counter-responsible at I3 ($-1.073$), mirroring
  SHAP's $\phi_X^Y = -0.625$
- DXY in the form "$R > 0$" fails for I3, but "$|R| > 0$" holds. Paper
  desiderata should switch to $|R|$ framing throughout (matches OBCB
  desiderata in `sec3_definitions.tex`).

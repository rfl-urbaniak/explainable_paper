### Section 3 closing — necessity/sufficiency decomposition

1. **Section-3 closing block** added: side-by-side comparison of PCI's
   necessity/sufficiency marginals with Pearl's PN/PS/PNS. Originally proposed between
   the Alice walkthrough and Table 2; moved at user direction to the end of Section 3
   so it sits between desiderata-verification and the Section 4 break.

2. **Corrected the PNS = PN·PS misclaim.** Initial draft asserted equality "in the
   individual-level case under independent residual noise"; user flagged this as false.
   Replaced with the correct relations
   `PNS = PN · P(Y_{x*}=y*) = PS · P(Y_{x'}=y')`,
   noting that `PNS = PN · PS` requires `Y_{x*} ⊥ Y_{x'}`, an assumption typically
   violated since the two potential outcomes share exogenous noise.

3. **Table 4 (`tab:pci_decomp`)** — 4-row 7-column table comparing Pearl's
   PN/PS/PNS with PCI's necessity/sufficiency marginals for both `gender` and
   `credit`, both Alice and Bob:

   | Person, Var | PN | PS | PNS | PCI nec | PCI suff | PCI |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Alice, gender | 0.045 | 1.000 | 0.045 | 0.394 | 0.667 | 0.263 |
   | Alice, credit | 0.180 | 1.000 | 0.180 | 0.298 | 0.667 | 0.199 |
   | Bob, gender   | 0.000 | ---   | 0.000 | 0.030 | 0.637 | 0.019 |
   | Bob, credit   | 0.900 | 0.955 | 0.860 | 0.188 | 0.637 | 0.119 |

   Bob gender PS undefined (conditioning event has prob 0). PCI marginals well-defined
   throughout because they integrate over $P_U$ rather than conditioning.

   structural information PN cannot register; Bob PN=0 vs PCI nec=0.030).

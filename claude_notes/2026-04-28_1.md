### PSCM insertion — APPROVED but NOT YET APPLIED

Text approved, rejected once for using "who/whom" (too anthropomorphic). Corrected gloss:

> \medskip\noindent\textit{Informally, a PSCM encodes which variables influence which others, through what mechanisms, and subject to what randomness. The structural equations specify each variable as a deterministic function of its causal parents and local noise; the exogenous distribution captures all irreducible uncertainty in the system. Together they make every counterfactual question well-posed and computable.}
>
> The OBCB model (Example~\ref{ex:obcb_stochastic}) is a PSCM with endogenous variables $\mathbf{V}=\{\varname{gender},\varname{credit},\varname{check},\varname{check\text{-}failed},\varname{loan}\}$, exogenous variables $\mathbf{U}=\{U_{\varname{gender}},U_{\varname{credit}},U_{\varname{check}},U_{\varname{loan}}\}$ drawn independently from $\mathrm{Unif}(0,1)$, and structural assignments
> \begin{align*}
> \varname{gender} &:= \mathbb{I}\{U_{\varname{gender}}\leq 0.5\},\qquad \varname{credit} := \mathbb{I}\{U_{\varname{credit}}\leq 0.5\},\\
> \varname{check} &:= \mathbb{I}\bigl\{U_{\varname{check}}\leq(1-\varname{gender})\cdot 0.2+\varname{gender}\cdot 0.9\bigr\},\\
> \varname{check\text{-}failed} &:= \varname{check}\cdot(1-\varname{credit}),\\
> \varname{loan} &:= \mathbb{I}\bigl\{U_{\varname{loan}}\leq\mathrm{loan\text{-}prob}[\varname{gender},\varname{check\text{-}failed}]\bigr\}\cdot\varname{check}.
> \end{align*}
> For Alice (\varname{gender}$=0$, \varname{credit}$=0$), the structural equation for \varname{check} assigns $P(\varname{check}=1)=0.2$: in 80\% of noise realizations she is not checked at all, making \varname{loan}$=0$ certain without \varname{credit} ever entering the causal chain. The PSCM makes this policy-determined bottleneck explicit and manipulable.

**Placement:** between PSCM `\end{definition}` and `\begin{definition}[Interventions]`.

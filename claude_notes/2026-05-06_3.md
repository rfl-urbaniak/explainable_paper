## Notebook (`docs/source/responsibility_archetypes.ipynb`)

- Renamed from `synthetic_design_c.ipynb` per user direction
  (`responsibility_archetypes` is more descriptive of content).
- Restructured to match the production style of
  `signal_mediation_computations.ipynb`: title with em-dash, summary
  blockquote, 9 numbered sections (Model and setup → Two factual cases →
  PCI → Desiderata → Sufficiency direction → Marginal SHAP → Causal SHAP →
  Comparison table → Reading the comparison) + Notes.
- Failure-mode-hypotheses cell dropped (debugging-only).
- DAG render function (`render_design_c`) upgraded to paper-quality
  graphviz: italic math labels with subscripts, color-coded by archetype
  (purple linear, blue overdetermined, green gated, grey irrelevant,
  orange double-circle outcome), white-ellipse mediators. Cell now also
  bakes `figures/design_c_dag.{pdf,png}` via `_project_root()` lookup.
- Causal SHAP added: explicit Shapley enumeration over $2^6 = 64$
  coalitions with do-intervention value function. On Design C
  (independent roots) coincides with marginal SHAP up to MC noise — the
  cell verifies this empirically (max diff $\sim$1.0 on $L_2$, $P$).
- Reading-the-comparison narrative settled on the **N/S decomposition** as
  the only methodological claim defensibly justified by the runs.
  Originally proposed three failures (no N/S, contestable-pair symmetry,
  preemption blindness); two of those did not hold up empirically:
  marginal SHAP correctly respects preemption on this model, and SHAP's
  asymmetry on the contestable $O$ pair tracks PCI's ranking by accident
  (deviation-from-prior-mean, not sufficiency). Only Tier-1 claim retained.

## Conceptual moves worth remembering

- **The impossible-cell phenomenon is a feature-set effect.** When the SCM's
  structural equations rule out some combinations of model inputs (here:
  $c=1$ rules out $cf=1$), promoting a structurally-determined variable to
  an observable model input creates a cell where $\tilde f$ is undefined.
  Causal SHAP's $do$-intervention can override the structural constraint and
  push the integrand into the undefined region.
- **PCI sidesteps this** because the witness mechanism does not promote
  `check_failed` to a feature; it conditions the alternative-world expectation
  on the witness rather than treating it as a player in the Shapley game.
- **The asymmetry between Alice ($cf^\star=0$) and Bob ($cf^\star=1$)**
  illustrates that the failure depends on which value is being held, not on
  some aggregate property of the example.
